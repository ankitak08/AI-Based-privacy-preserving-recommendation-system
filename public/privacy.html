Privacy

This project is designed to avoid storing personal content data.

Stored per user:
- user_id (random internal id)
- username + password hash (for login)
Not stored:
- exact song titles
- browsing history
- real audio
- email/phone/address
- mood history (mood is inferred in-session to generate recommendations)
